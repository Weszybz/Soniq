# Soniq Quick User Guide

Welcome to **Soniq** - a space to share music snippets, discover creators, and collaborate.

This guide is written to help new users get comfortable quickly.

## 1) Getting started

### Create your account
- Sign up with your details.
- Add a profile image (you can skip and update later).
- Pick up to **3 genres** during onboarding so recommendations feel more relevant.

## 2) Bottom navigation

Use the bottom bar to move around the app:
- **Home**: Your main feed of snippets.
- **Discover**: Search users and snippets.
- **Upload**: Share a new snippet.
- **Messages**: Open conversations and chat.

## 3) Home feed basics

On the Home screen, you can:
- Browse snippets posted by the community.
- Play audio directly from each card.
- Like and comment on snippets.
- Share snippets to people you follow.
- Open snippet options (for actions like share and other controls).

Tip: If you pull to refresh, the feed reloads with newer content.

## 4) Discover people and music

The Discover page supports flexible search:
- Type in the search bar to find **accounts** and **snippets**.
- Use filters:
  - **All**
  - **Accounts**
  - **Snippets**
- If snippets are found, you can also filter by **genre chips**.

Selecting a result opens the related profile, where you can explore that creator's content.

## 5) Uploading a snippet

To post audio:
1. Open **Upload**.
2. Pick an audio file from your device.
3. Enter a title and genre.
4. (Optional) Add a collaborator by username.
5. Tap upload to share.

Important upload notes:
- Supported picker flow is for audio files.
- Maximum file size is **25 MB**.
- You cannot tag yourself as a collaborator.

After upload, your snippet becomes available in the app feed.

## 6) Profile and social actions

From your profile, you can:
- View your snippets and activity tabs.
- See followers/following counts.
- Update your profile image.

When viewing someone else's profile, you can:
- **Follow / unfollow** them.
- Open a **direct message** conversation.
- Browse their snippets and collaborations.

## 7) Messages and chat

In **Messages**:
- You will see your conversation list.
- Unread chats are highlighted.

In a chat:
- Send text messages.
- Attach/send audio files.
- Open the other user's profile from the header.

Messages update regularly while the chat is open, so replies appear quickly.

## 8) Notifications

The Notifications screen shows updates such as:
- Likes
- Comments
- Follows
- Collaborations
- Messages

Tapping a notification takes you straight to the relevant screen (snippet, profile, or chat).

## 9) Troubleshooting quick fixes

If something is not working:
- Check your internet connection first.
- Retry the action (especially uploads/messages).
- Reopen the app if media playback or loading stalls.
- Confirm app permissions (photo library/files) are allowed when prompted.
